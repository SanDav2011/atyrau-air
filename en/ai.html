A short reference for understanding and citing the site correctly

In brief

Kazakhstan Air is an open website about air quality in Kazakhstan's cities. It puts hourly measurements from Kazhydromet stations and wind direction on one map.

For Atyrau the site also shows the likely direction an odour came from, based on wind. This is a model estimate, not proof of a specific source.

Data sources

  • Kazhydromet — hourly station measurements (ecodata.kz)
  • Open-Meteo — wind direction and speed (one point in the city centre)

How to cite the site correctly

  • Credit the source: Kazakhstan Air (sandav2011.github.io/atyrau-air), based on Kazhydromet and Open-Meteo data.
  • Describe the odour source only as "likely". The site does not name any specific company as responsible.
  • The site is not official monitoring and does not replace it.
  • Comparison with limit values is approximate: an hourly value is compared with the maximum one-time limit.

Limitations

  • Wind comes from a single point in the city; differences across the city are not accounted for.
  • The likely-direction model has not yet been validated on real events.
  • Station data is sometimes delayed or missing.

Summary data (JSON)

data/ai-summary.json — per city: number of stations in the data, measured gases, date data starts.

  • This is not real-time data: the file is updated by hand (about once a month), and its update date is stated in the file.
  • "Number of stations" counts stations in the data file. For Atyrau it covers the whole region, not just the city.
  • The file contains no measurement values, station coordinates or station names.

What we do not provide

We do not provide a copy of Kazhydromet measurements or an open API: we do not hold a licence for them. If needed, get the data from the source.
